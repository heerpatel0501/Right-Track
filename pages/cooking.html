🍳 Cooking Skills

📌 What is Cooking?

Cooking is a combination of skill, creativity, and knowledge of ingredients. It allows you to prepare healthy meals, explore cultures, and express creativity through food.

🍛 Indian Daily Meals

These are simple and essential meals for everyday life:

  • Roti + Sabzi → Basic home meal
  • Dal Rice → Protein-rich and healthy
  • Khichdi → Easy and light food

🇮🇳 Regional Dishes

  • Gujarati → Dhokla, Thepla, Kadhi
  • Punjabi → Paneer Butter Masala, Paratha
  • South Indian → Dosa, Idli, Sambhar

Learning regional food helps you understand culture and flavors.

🍔 Fast Food & Snacks

  • Sandwich, Maggi, Pasta
  • Burgers and wraps

Quick recipes are useful for busy schedules and beginners.

🥪 Veg Sandwich

Steps:

  • Take 2 bread slices
  • Add butter, cucumber, tomato
  • Sprinkle salt & chat masala
  • Grill or serve fresh

🍜 Maggi

Steps:

  • Boil 1.5 cups water
  • Add Maggi noodles
  • Add tastemaker
  • Cook for 2–3 mins

🍝 Pasta

Steps:

  • Boil pasta in water
  • Add sauce + veggies
  • Mix well and cook
  • Serve hot
