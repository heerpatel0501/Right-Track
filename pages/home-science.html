🏠 Home Science

📌 What is Home Science?

Home Science is a field that focuses on managing home, health, food, clothing, and family life. It combines science and daily life skills to improve lifestyle and well-being.

💡 Areas of Home Science

  • Nutrition & Food Science
  • Textile & Clothing
  • Human Development
  • Home Management

🥗 Food & Nutrition

Focuses on healthy eating habits, balanced diet, and proper nutrition. It helps in maintaining good health and preventing diseases.

🏡 Home Management

Includes organizing daily tasks, managing time, maintaining cleanliness, and creating a comfortable living environment.

👗 Textile & Clothing

Involves fabric selection, clothing design, and understanding fashion basics.

🔥 Skills You Learn

  • Cooking and nutrition planning
  • Time and resource management
  • Creativity in home decoration
  • Basic health and hygiene practices

🚀 How to Start Learning

  • Learn basic cooking and nutrition
  • Maintain daily routine at home
  • Explore simple DIY and decoration ideas
  • Watch tutorials and practice regularly

🌍 Real Life Use

Home Science helps in daily life by improving health, managing time, and maintaining a better lifestyle. It is useful for personal growth and professional careers like nutritionist, designer, or teacher.
