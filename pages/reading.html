📖 Reading

📌 What is Reading?

Reading is the process of understanding written words and gaining knowledge. It helps improve vocabulary, imagination, and thinking skills. Reading can include books, articles, blogs, and digital content.

🚀 Benefits of Reading

  • Improves knowledge and understanding
  • Enhances vocabulary and language skills
  • Boosts imagination and creativity
  • Reduces stress and improves focus

📚 Types of Reading

  • Books (Fiction & Non-fiction)
  • Newspapers & Articles
  • Self-help & Educational content
  • Digital reading (blogs, e-books)

🔥 Reading Habits & Sessions

Start with small reading sessions like 10–15 minutes daily. Gradually increase your time and explore different genres. Consistency helps build a strong reading habit.

🌍 Real Life Benefits

Reading helps in academics, communication, and personal growth. It improves focus, builds confidence, and gives you a deeper understanding of the world. Successful people often make reading a daily habit.
