📌 What is Coding?

Coding is the process of giving instructions to a computer to perform tasks. It is used to build websites, mobile apps, games, and software. Every digital product you use today is created using coding.

🚀 Why Coding is Important

  • High demand skill in today’s world
  • Used in AI, apps, websites, and games
  • Improves problem solving & logical thinking
  • Opens strong career opportunities
