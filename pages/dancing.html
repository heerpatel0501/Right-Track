💃 Dance

📌 What is Dance?

Dance is a form of expression using movement and rhythm. It combines music, body coordination, and creativity to express emotions and tell stories.

🚀 Benefits of Dance

  • Improves fitness and flexibility
  • Boosts confidence and expression
  • Reduces stress and improves mood
  • Enhances coordination and balance

🎭 Types of Dance

  • Classical (Bharatanatyam, Kathak)
  • Hip-Hop & Street Dance
  • Contemporary
  • Freestyle

🔥 Learning & Practice

Start with basic steps and practice regularly. Follow tutorials, learn different styles, and focus on rhythm and body movement.

🌍 Real Life Benefits

Dance helps in staying active, expressing emotions, and building confidence. It is used in performances, competitions, fitness routines, and entertainment.
