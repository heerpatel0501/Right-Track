📱 Digital Marketing

📌 What is Digital Marketing?

Digital Marketing is the promotion of products, services, or brands using online platforms like social media, search engines, websites, and email. It helps businesses reach a larger audience through the internet.

🚀 Why Digital Marketing is Important

  • High demand career skill
  • Helps businesses grow online
  • Reaches global audience
  • Opens freelancing & earning opportunities

💡 Types of Digital Marketing

  • Social Media Marketing (Instagram, Facebook)
  • Search Engine Optimization (SEO)
  • Content Marketing (blogs, videos)
  • Email Marketing

🔥 Learning & Practice

Start by learning basics like social media posting, content creation, and SEO. Practice by creating your own page or blog and analyzing growth. Consistency and creativity are key in digital marketing.

🌍 Real World Use

Digital marketing is used by businesses, influencers, and brands to grow their presence online. It plays a major role in advertising, branding, and customer engagement in today’s digital world.
